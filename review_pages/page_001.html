    I found myself at Ma Poule Mouillée as part of a food tour I was on. We were not told in advance which places we were visiting, so it was a pleasant surprise to find out that this was on the route. While I didn't know too much about this spot, I was aware of its popularity and that they served good poutine; needless to say, I was looking forward to checking it out.

    Ma Poule Mouillée is a Portuguese restatuant that specializes in chicken and poutine. We were there late morning so it was not crowded, though I was told it's normal for a line for form around the block during peak times. Our tour guide also mentioned that this place, along with La Banquise which sits catty corner, are two of the most popular places for poutine in the city.

    We were here for poutine - specifically, we got the variety topped with chorizo and roasted chicken. It was delicious - the fries were crispy, and the combo for the chorizo and chicken with the gravy and cheese curds really made for an elevated poutine. We didn't have time for anything else, but what we had was enough to make an impression.

    Overall, Ma Poule Mouillée was a total yes - this was one of the best poutines I've had in a while and I would definitely return the next time I'm in Montreal.
