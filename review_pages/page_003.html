    To avoid long lines, I got here at 10am. Also, make sure you push the door to enter, don't be like me and pull :) I knew what I wanted, so I got #8, small with couple drinks, egg tart and blonde donut thing.

    The poutine was decent. I liked the chicken, it was moist, seasoned well and I would been perfectly fine just ordering the chicken by itself. The whole plate as a whole, few things they could done better.

    I wish they added more gravy... if they layered it, it would have helped. After the fries, add the gravy, then the cheese, chicken and sausage and another layer of the gravy. After you eat the top, once you get to the rest of the fries, there's no gravy leftover unfortunately. I don't know if you can ask for additional gravy on the side or if you need to pay for additional, but it would have helped to have more gravy.

    The interior was small, but plenty of seating to enjoy your food. Egg tart was decent, but it would been a lot better if served warm.
