    Our one and only Canadian poutine experience was a bit of a let down. We came last night, and luckily there was no line, so we ordered--ahem, *tried* to order--the poutine with chicken and chorizo and a small green salad. One of us speaks French, and was literally enunciating in a crystal clear manner, "POUTINE AVEC POULET ET CHORIZO" but they were completely befuddled and then tried to claim that they didn't make a small salad, which was right there on their menu, which one of us pointed out. So, not the most welcome introduction or experience.

    You work your way down the line and pay; the woman at the register was literally standing there eating a yogurt while we were waiting, so don't set those customer service standards too high--it doesn't get better. When we went to go sit down, every single spot was completely filthy and we had to wipe down the tables ourselves.

    Getting to the actual food: the salad was an afterthought, with a heap of dressing tossed on top in a pool, so, non merci. The "celebrated" poutine was only OK. The chorizo tasted cold and bland and more like a hot dog. We liked the gravy, but it seemed like there was a trace of something sweet in it which we did not appreciate. The chicken was good, but there were massively large and unappetizing pieces of chicken fat thrown in there, and everything was just drowning in the curds and sauce, etc. It was too much, and not in a good way.

    We were debating whether to go to the other famous place across the street and really wish that we had! Do NOT recommend. 2.5 stars, rounding down.
