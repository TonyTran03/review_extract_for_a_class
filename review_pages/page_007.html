    My friend and I were stuck in Montreal and were recommended by a Canadian friend to go try some "well-known" poutine. I've never been a fan of poutine in the States, but fully acknowledge I've probably never had high-quality poutine. We read the reviews for this shop and fully expected the long wait. Service was fast and NOT rude (we read many reviews saying they were rude), and we made sure to grab some egg tarts on the way out the door. We ordered the #8 poutine half size and did not regret it.

    Overall, the poutine was incredibly savory - would highly recommend getting a salad or soda to cut the salt. Each item in the poutine was quality! Fries were crisp (not soggy under the gravy), gravy had a nice flavor, chicken was moist and the skin was a nice touch, and cheese curds were tasty with a nice chew and flavor. Very busy inside on a Sunday afternoon, most folks order and grab takeout. Lots of families too.

    Friend's Review: "The chicken was the best thing. Just go and get the chicken."
