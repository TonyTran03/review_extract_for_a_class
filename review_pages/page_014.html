    I arrived in Montreal for the first time in 5 years and this is the first place I went. Brilliant choice because it started my trip off VERY right.

    La Banquise is across the street from this establishment and many will say that's the best poutine in Montreal but they would be wrong. VERY wrong. The best poutine is at Ma Poule Mouillee and for $12, you get a whole lot of it so bring your friends and your appetites! Chicken, sausage, curds, fries, gravy, sauces, delicious!

    Everything else is great. Their chicken is delicious and for me, superior to one of their main competitors, Romados. It's just so flavourful and juicy and everything you'd want from Portuguese chicken.

    The salad and potatoes are also solid. I really don't think you could go wrong with anything here, especially when you consider that they are quite inexpensive. They have a knack for flavours and they deliver excellence every time.

    Salad, a whole chicken, a large poutine, and side order of potatoes - enough for 3 hungry people and leftovers all for $36

    Ma Poule Mouillée was recommended by many people as one of the best poutine places in Montreal. However the wordplay in the name was not true. Maybe there wasn't enough foreplay but it was not "mouillée" (wet/juicy).

    To begin, the line was extremely disorganized. Staff inside were inattentive and were too busy talking with one another than actually helping out customers. A simple pickup order that we called in in advance took over 25 minutes to pick up!

    The poutine itself was fairly generous as its stuffed with fries, cheese, chicken, sausage topped with gravy. However, everything was fairly cold, luke-warm at best. A good poutine should be piping hot leaving you with a slight burn on the roof of your mouth.

    The chicken was lacking in flavour and dryer than your crush from grade 6. I'd even say the rotisserie chicken at Costco packs more of a punch. And don't get me started on how much cartilage and bone I had in the poutine as well. Choking from a poutine is probably one of the last things you'd expect but guess you can never be too careful.

    I had really high expectations for this place and unfortunately it was a let down. I'm hoping this was an isolated incident as others seem to have had a great experience. But if you're doing a food crawl for the best poutine in MTL, this wasn't it.

    2.4/5

    I think I made a mistake trying to be healthy(ish) here by ordering the grilled chicken and chorizo salad (Salade Mathilde). It was HUGE! And I think there was way too much meat for it to truly qualify as a "salad." It's probably meant to be shared between 2 people or one very hungry athlete!

    There was a good amount of lettuce and freshly-cut onions, carrots, and peppers, as well as a generous amount of sliced chorizo and grilled chicken (looked like both light and dark meat). It was definitely good value for less than $15 with tax.

    There were a few things that didn't make this the ideal dish to get here, however... While the chicken was nicely marinated, I felt there was a bit of a flavor clash between the chicken, chorizo, and salad dressing. Potatoes would probably have paired better with these meats.

    Also, the chicken tasted a bit cold and dry. I saw them cut it fresh, but I think they had grabbed it from a pile of ready-made chicken that was already off the grill.

    Overall, great value, quick service, and friendly staff, but I'd maybe recommend prioritizing the warm dishes rather than the entrée salad.

    Salade Mathilde
