    I had a difficult time figuring out which Portuguese place to hit up with my limited time in Montreal. Should I go to good 'ol reliable, favourite Romados or try one of the new players in town? After much debate, I decided to check out Ma Pouille Mouillée as I kept on reading so many great things about them.

    Hours are bomb. Everyday open early and late till 10:00pm! The menu is up on the wall and prices already includes all the taxes!

    Stand in line and order. It's like an assembly line. As you make your way down, the till is at the end where you can grab drinks and desserts.

    - Le Louis Cyr (#2) ($11.00) - half a chicken with fries and salad. Portion size is huge! A mountainous amount of fries and chicken. I should mention that I attempted to eat all this by myself - don't try this at home folks.

    Make sure you go to at least one of the many, many Portuguese places while you're in town.

    Glad we "chickened out" and tried Ma Poule Mouillée. With a fun shop name, this is now among the most popular Portuguese chicken rotisseries in Montréal. Despite no lineup in the mid-afternoon, it was still quite packed with happy diners & folks ordering takeout.

    A whiff of aroma came from the rotisserie as it turned open whole chickens in unison. Staff at the counter happily processed food orders. For takeout, I had their famous poutine with Sãn Jorge cheese, chorizo & grilled chicken. At the pastry display, I couldn't help but try their Portuguese egg tarts (natas). A collective assembly of the shop's best offerings!

    Egg tarts were loaded with sweet doughy custard and a crackly crust composed of flaky layers. The packed poutine was oozing from the container with toppings. Its pulled chicken was moist with seasoned slow-cooked grill flavour, which was more noticeable than the chorizo slices. It blended well in melted cheeses, gravy & spicy sauce over the fries.

    I noticed the saltiness of Quebec food along with its bold flavours. For $12, it's amazing that the "small" poutine was enough for two meals. Bravo to the owner Tony for turning his passion for Portuguese chicken into one of the most popular cheap eat-outs in town.

    When I am in Montreal, I eat...and eat...and eat....I try as many restaurants as I can, sometimes, just a small sample of each...just so I can savor the particular flavors....

    I had heard about this chicken place and I needed to try it. It's a casual place with a funky menu, so you can pick a meal by the "name".

    Opened to the public since July 29, 2013, Ma Poule Mouillée is a great casual restaurant on Le Plateau Mont-Royal.

    The history goes like this.....from a young age, Chief Tony Alves was working with his uncle who had a restaurant. From that moment he permeated every movement his uncle baker made and learned every he could. With a dream and a clear idea in mind, Tony Alves did not hesitate a second to open his own restaurant. From then on, chef Alves learned to conceive a very special spicy sauce. For, as the chef says, it is the sauce that makes the difference, because everyone is able to grill a chicken.

    I really enjoyed this delicious Portuguese chicken roasted on charcoal.
