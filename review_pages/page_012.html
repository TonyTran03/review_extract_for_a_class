    This was a very satisfying lunch. We ordered the Chicken sandwiches, Half chicken, Poutine, and Portuguese tart. The roasted chicken had a deliciously fragrant and flavorful skin, and the meat was juicy and full of flavor. The kitchen's on-site roasting technique really added to the flavor of the chicken.

    The chicken sandwiches were also very juicy and flavorful. The Portuguese tart was more solid compared to the soft, warm, custardy ones found in Asia, but both are unique and worth trying.

    The poutine was a classic sausages version and met the expected standard. The portion sizes were very generous, and we couldn't finish it all. So, if you have to choose, definitely start with their chicken, which is really flavorful and juicy despite being dry.

    Overall, it was a great lunch, and we highly recommend trying the chicken and Portuguese tart.
