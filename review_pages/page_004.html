    Ma Poule Mouillee is a place where dreams come true. After reading Maria's review back in 2020, I knew I had to visit this spot if I ever found myself in Montreal. Well, I am so glad I did! We visited for lunch and there was a line around the block. Now with a ton of amazing spots in Montreal, you may be thinking is it worth waiting in line? Yes, yes it most certainly is. Once you make your way inside they have a great assembly line going. You order, then make your way down to the register. By the time you make it to the register, your order is waiting for you. Here is the time to grab extra sauce, we did not and I was regretful.

    The Portuguese chicken was moist and delicious, their sauce is just so freaking good. We also ordered the petit poutine, and let me tell you nothing was petite about that thing. My friend and I could barely make a dent in it. But it was also so flavorful and delicious. We rounded things out with some orange Sumol (the best flavor) and a pastel de nata.

    They have a variety of bar seating/tables around the restaurant. Even with the long line we were able to find seats to eat without an issue.

    If I'm ever back in Montreal, I will never not go here. The best!
