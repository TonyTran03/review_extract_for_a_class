    We saw so many good reviews for this place and walked by pretty often, so we knew we had to try it.

    As there was no English menu, we struggled a bit to figure out what to order but it all worked out with us eating well in the end.

    Once you enter, you go up to the counter to order and you watch them chop up your meat before you pay at the cash register.

    The chorizo didn't taste like regular chorizo -- it had somewhat of a tomato-based flavor and was more dense (less chewy) than I expected. Still yummy though, just different.

    The chicken (as everyone else in the reviews have said) is amazing -- super moist and tender and delicious. The sauce is incredibly greasy, but hey, that's definitely what makes it so good! Just make sure to grab a bunch of napkins to eat it with (ask the cashier for plenty)!

    Chorizo and whole portugese chicken

    My mother and I were stalling from checking into our AirBnB so we stumbled upon this place. Poutine is the food symbol of Montreal and so, my mother and I must of course sample it, and this place was very promising. I expected a long line and there was. We were thankfully not forced to stand outside, but the line was to the door, though that may be because of the set up of the restaurant that had people standing in line with the door. The line moves fairly quickly though as the people working here have a great assembly process that you get to watch live. There is inside seating, though with the busyness of the place, I wouldn't rule out ordering to go.

    I opted to order the "Bon, les Portugais Font de la Poutine," which I called the "Number 8" because I was afraid of butchering my French pronunciation in my first day back in a Francophone area. I ordered the large which was a large plate that was plenty to fill the stomachs of both me and my mother. The cheese bits were tasty; the gravy was rich and soaked the fries well so that I didn't have any plain fries (plain fries are a a bit of a nightmare for me by the way); the chicken and sausage offered a meaty dimension to the poutine. It was a decent poutine! The last bite was amazing because I amassed enough cheese with the meat and potato that created an excellent flavor and texture combination. You can opt for a pitcher of water for the table if you don't buy the water bottles (which you shouldn't because water should always be free).

    The menu looks fabulous and if I had more time up here, I would come back to try the other options.

    Best chicken, fries, salad meal in Montreal.

    The crown used to go to Romados which is still amazingly good but MPM is a notch better.

    Tony Alves the owner used to work at Romados for 20 years. His defection would be similar to if Tom Brady and Bill Belichek left the Patriots for elsewhere (damm I sure do not like Brady).

    ******local tip #1 ******Come early if you don't have patience for long lines. I mean real real early. We came at 4:30pm on a Saturday and it was already 5-10 people out the door. When we left at 5:30 pm it was around 20-25 deep in line. I've seen it 40-50 deep at 6pm.

    The chicken is moist, fall off the bone and charcoal grilled delish. Over 200 reviews have stated that so no need to continue. The fries are equally good. The salad does get lost under a mountain of chicken and fries but it's good to have something healthy on this gargantuan fat man's plate.

    Dining is simple. Don't come expecting much. The volume of food they sell through here is insane. I had courteous service and the restaurant felt like a sweltering inferno on an October day.

    *****local tip#2***** your hair and clothes will absorb the smell as will the leftovers in your car. Don't wear those Sunday church clothes.

    *****local tip#3**** if you like spicy ask for extra orange hot sauce. They paint it on with a brush and it's what makes this place a notch over Romados.

    Enjoy to the max. Live to eat don't eat to live!
