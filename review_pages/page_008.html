    There's lots of hype on social media posted about Ma Poule Mouillee and I was excited to try it. I was a bit skeptical if it would actually live up to its name. Goodness I was pleasantly surprised to find that this was indeed a very moist fire roasted Portuguese chicken. I am always reluctant to order chicken in an establishment as it's oftentimes dry and overcooked. In my opinion condiments and sauces should enhance what you are eating, not be a necessary addition to revive your dish.
    If you arrive during peak dining hours, be prepared to wait on a line that extends outside of the eatery. It does go quick though cause the staff behind the counters are efficient like well oiled machinery.
    I ordered a half chicken with fries and salad, two orders of a chicken quarter which also came with the same sides as well as a large poutine with chicken, chorizo, cheese curds and gravy. You are also asked if you'd like a mild or spicy sauce which they top onto your chicken. For my after dinner goodie, I got traditional flan, coconut flan as well as a cream cannoli for good measure.
    They had a nice selection of Portuguese desserts and breads. Additionally I saw two types of flan, and Portuguese egg tarts as well as typical French goodies yum!
    Entrees are packed in to go boxes as well as your side salads. That is a bonus as I do not like my cold mixed salad greens in vinaigrette touching my hot food. The portions here are generous and you more than likely will take your leftovers to go.
    There is decent indoor seating for this cozy establishment. Although every seat was taken, by the time I placed my order I was able to secure a table.
    Be aware that if you are a serious condiment lover, such as myself they do not give you more than one or two tiny cups per entree. When I asked for another small portion of their house made sauce the lady at the counter charged me a dollar. Not sure that is the case for everyone though cause there were plenty others asking for extra. I am guessing it depends on who is at the checkout counter?
    All in all it was a nice experience and the chicken was very memorable especially the chicken chorizo poutine which they are known for. I would eat there again for sure.

    I came to Montreal during restaurant week and out of all of the delicious meals I had during my stay, this one stood out above the rest.
    I had seen a picture online of the fish that I wanted, a #8, which is a rotisserie chicken and chorizo poutine, I opted for "hot" (more piquant) sauce as well. I opted for the small size as I wanted to eat a lot more that day and I'm honestly glad I did. I can eat a lot of food but the amount that they packed into that small, aluminum container nearly had me bursting at the gills; cheese curds are no joke!
    This was one of those meals where you take a bite and are immediately in another world, where nothing else matters, and all you want to do is keep eating. At first, I was devouring my meal but as it quickly started to disappear, I knew I had to savour it and make this moment last as long as I could.
    The inside, though small, had comfortable seating, with plenty of views of the surrounding neighborhood. Though I thought this place would be touristy, I was eating with locals and construction workers. Sitting there eating, I felt like I knew a secret that other travelers did not.
    For dessert, I got two pasteis de natas to really still down that Portuguese flavor.
    Do yourself a favor and come here. I am honestly kicking myself that I didn't make it here twice during my four day stay.
