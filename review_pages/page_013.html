    When in Montreal Poutine is a must! One that truly stood out in my research for great poutine was Ma Poule Mouilee!! The restaurant is popular with constant lineup, but they are very quick. I got in within 15 mins wait. What is unique about this place is they don't make any poutine but one that is topped with freshly grilled Portuguese chicken shreds and chorizo too! It was absolutely poutine heaven! The grilled chicken was so tender and juicy and there was so much gravy and cheese curd every single bite of fries had just enough of it! Portion was HUGE! With the regular size for $18 the two of us barely managed to finish it! They do have a petite option too that is not so petite at all and would be great for sharing too for those who like to eat lighter. Not only do they have poutine and chicken but several Portuguese desserts too! I was very tempted but had to hold back because I was just too full. This will definitely be a place I would revisit when I come to Montreal again.
