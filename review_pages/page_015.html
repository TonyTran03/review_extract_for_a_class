    I completely forgot that Ma Poule was on the Netflix show, Somebody Feed Phil until I was in line and heard someone mentioned it. I initially came because I had read reviews about Portuguese egg tarts - aside from reviewing boba places around the world, I'm also down to try Portuguese egg tarts everywhere I go (if it's sold).
    Anyways, to get back on topic - Ma Poule is fast paced! I ordered number 14 and asked to not have fries. Which was a mistake on my part. The staff seems to speak more French than English. She thought I meant to add the Poutine, which they're known for. I then tried to explain in my limited french that what I meant was no poutine and also no fries with the chicken. That lead to even more confusion as she couldn't believe I would just not accept the free fries that came with the chicken. TLDR - I just accepted my fate of the large amount of fries that came with the chicken.

    The chicken is phenomenal, there's no denying it. I recommend that you should have someone on standby who LOVES fries because half the box was just fries. My friend and I were shocked by the quantity. Sadly, the Portuguese egg tart did not live up to people's review. Too sugary and too chewy, like they didn't beat the cream enough or something. I was really disappointed. Skip the egg tart but not the chicken!
