    It was very good. We got the half chicken, fries and salad that is so popular, and also the poutine, and it didn't disappoint. The line was long, but it moved quickly. We got it to go and ate in the park nearby.

    We center our trips around food, and this is what I'll tell you. It is a very good place, but not mind-blowing legendary like the hype wants you to beleive. I believe the issue is the large flow of people. I think the place can make very good chicken, but with the huge lines of people, the process has become a massive assembly line. Chicken, fries, on a plate, now you pay and off you go. Next person! There is not any attention to detail and the sauce and flavoring was not that great. Good, but not great. The chicken had very little of the sauce that they claim is so great.

    We went to a small place the next night. It is a Portuguese family owned place called Portugalia near Mont Royal Park, that was mostly food to go. The guy made the chicken to order on a charcoal grill and asked if you wanted the sauce mild or spicy and took the time to make sure the chicken was properly sauced and grilled. It was hands-down better than MPM! We then took our food to the park for a feast.

    My advice is try MPM for the experience, but if you really want better chicken, go to a smaller place where there is more attention to detail.

    My boyfriend and I went to Montreal over New Years, and this was one of the only places on his "must try" list that was open and ohhh GOODNESS am I glad it was. In fact, we opted to go to Ma Poule Mouillée in lieu of a more *traditional* poutine. We got the "small poutine" (which was anything but small) and Le Louis Cyr (which is a half chicken with French fries and a fresh green salad).

    Both dishes were out of this world. The signature spicy sauce was featured in both-- it's tangy, spicy, warm, and, in the poutine, the cheese curds perfectly complimented it.

    The place is small, but we got there at just the right time-- the line wasn't especially long, but the inside was PACKED. However, because of the nature of the place, diner turnover is pretty quick-- we were able to snag a window seat and watch a quieter part of this beautiful city.
