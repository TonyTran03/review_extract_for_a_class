    We walked by this place on our way to the park and saw a line outside, so naturally I looked it up to find out it was a Portuguese roast chicken place. I've never had it before and decided to give their takeout a try when we got the chance.

    My friend and I came by to pick up some dinner on Saturday around 7:30, and it wasn't too crowded. They have some tables for seating, but it's nothing fancy as this is a fast casual type of place. Looking around, I noticed that all food, whether it's dine-in or to go, is served in takeout containers.

    They serve their chicken in a variety of ways - by itself, with side of fries and salad, in poutine, etc. We ordered half a chicken ($8.27) as well as a small poutine ($9.57). Typically their poutine uses both chicken and chorizo, but we asked for chicken only to accommodate my friend's diet.

    I definitely liked the roast chicken more than the poutine. The skin was very flavorful, just not as crispy as I like since the chicken was served with some juice in the container. I was also expecting a more fragrant roasted flavor.

    The poutine was just okay... keep in mind, though, that I am not a poutine kinda person, and we didn't eat it until maybe 30 minutes after getting it, so the fries were a bit soggy by the time we ate. My favorite part of this dish was the gravy.

    I enjoyed the chicken and am glad we tried it, but it wasn't different enough from other roast chicken for me to come back.

    Came here for dinner after a road trip from Toronto. There was a huge line out the door, even as there was freezing rain and 20°F weather. I already wrote this spot down when I researched places to eat in Montréal, although a convenience store owner nearby also named this shop as a recommendation.

    We waited around 20 minutes in line. Inside was packed, probably due to the weather, but also it just seems like a really popular spot to eat chicken. I noticed a lot of people were eating family style.

    We ordered at the counter and picked up our food as we paid by the cashier. We got a half chicken, a couple of salads, and a poutine. We got the egg tarts for dessert. Everything came out quickly. The bag and containers itself was quite oily.

    The chicken and poutine were incredibly salty. It was distracting from the actual flavor of the food. It's a good thing that we ordered those side salads because I don't think I would've gotten through the meal without it. The portion was also more than enough for our small family to share. I wish the meat was juicier as well.

    The best thing we got was the egg tarts.

    I don't know if it was bad luck, but I would not go here again based on this experience alone. I hope it was bad luck. It was not worth waiting in the freezing rain for.
