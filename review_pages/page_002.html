    Hands down the best poutine fusion in Montreal. The Portugese chicken compliments the poutine very well and definitely a dark horse in Montreal. They don't seem to get as much attention as some other poutine places, but don't sleep on this experience.

    For their price, it is a lot of food, one large serving of their 19$ grilled chicken with sausage and poutine is a great meal that will last you the entire day, it was more than enough for two. They debone two legs of chicken on top of the poutine that they pulled fresh off their grill!

    Service was fast, there is plenty of room to eat at the restaurant and they have a separate counter for the pastries. We had to get some of their Portuguese tarts (10$ for 6) and they were also amazing. Creamy, custardy, perfectly baked. It reheated well in the air fryer when we brought some back as souvenirs to enjoy.

    This is a place worth stopping by in Montreal, poutine in one hand and a batch of tarts for dessert on the other.
